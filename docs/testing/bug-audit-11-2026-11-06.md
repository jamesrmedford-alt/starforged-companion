  payoffs, upgrades and suffer, narration and context, fact continuity and the
  World Journal, entities and character, session / clocks / safety, sectors /
  oracles / truths, multiplayer / audio / logs, UI / settings / help), a
  cross-cutting sweep over the whole `src/` tree for the repo's recurring
  invariant violations (`rules/reachability.md`, `rules/foundry-api.md`,
  CLAUDE.md's architecture constraints), and a rules-fidelity pass comparing the
  module's mechanics with the vendored foundry-ironsworn data (v1.27.0) and the
  play-kit document — plus the lead's own pass. Each auditor was pointed first
  at the 54 commits since the tenth audit's base `8fb7bfa` (the fixes for issues
  #335–#338 and the open items fixed after them, and Batch Mode, issue #340:
  82 `src/` files, +11,739 / −2,013 lines — the least-reviewed code in the tree)
  and then at its whole area, tracing each entry point (chat commands, card
  buttons, hooks, dialogs, relays, panels, settings) against the rules text, the
  repo's invariants and `docs/decisions.md`, and writing a probe for each defect.
- Batch Mode had an auditor of its own: about 3,300 new lines (`src/batch/*`,
  `src/ui/roundWindow.js`) on top of the move pipeline's split into
  `src/moves/movePipeline.js`. Every other auditor also checked its own area's
  paths through a round, so most Batch Mode rows were reached from two or three
  sides.
- Every candidate was re-verified by the lead: every probe file was run (each
  fails on its own finding's assertion — none on an import error, a timeout or a
  setup crash — with its controls passing), the cited source was read for the
  rows the lead checked by hand (Z01, Z02, Z11 and Z13 among them), and every
  row was checked against `docs/known-issues.md`'s open items, `docs/decisions.md`
  and the tenth report's excluded and checked lists. 61 rows whose
  status turned on intent, a rules reading, overlap with a known item or their
  severity were handed to four independent adversarial refuters (see "Refuter
  pass").
- Same-shape findings from several auditors were merged into one row: the 104
  candidates became 74 findings and 8 decisions, and one row (two
  candidates) was refuted and dropped with its probes. A merged row keeps every
  auditor's probe; the area notes keep the merged-away rows' own text.
- This is a source and harness audit, not a live Foundry playthrough. Rows
  marked **(Foundry)** depend on behaviour that could not be checked offline.
