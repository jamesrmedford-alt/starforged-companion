## How this audit ran

- Eleven area auditors (one per `src/` area, the whole tree) and a lead
  cross-cutting pass. Each auditor traced its area's entry points — chat
  commands, card buttons, hooks, dialogs — against the rules text
  (`docs/rules-reference/`, the vendored move and asset data under
  `vendor/foundry-ironsworn/json-packs/`), the repo's own invariants
  (`rules/reachability.md`, `rules/foundry-api.md`, `rules/narrator-memory.md`)
  and `docs/decisions.md`, and wrote a probe for each defect it found.
- Every candidate was then re-verified by the lead against the source at HEAD
  (the cited lines and their callers), run, and checked against
  `docs/known-issues.md`'s open items and `docs/decisions.md`. The seventeen
  findings whose status turned on intent or a rules reading were each handed
  to an independent adversarial refuter (see "Refuter pass" at the end).
- Same-shape findings from several auditors were merged into one row.
- This is a source and harness audit, not a live Foundry playthrough. Rows
  marked **(Foundry)** or **(API)** depend on behaviour that could not be
  checked offline.

## Evidence standard

- **Where** gives `path:line` at `39331b4` for the defect and, where there is
  one, the other side of the hop.
- **Reproduced** names the probe (without its area folder or the `.probe.js`
  suffix) and the failing assertion's actual vs expected. *traced* rows have
  no probe; the chain was followed by hand.
- The probes live under `tests/probes/<area>/` as `*.probe.js`, so
  `npm test` never collects them. Run them with

  ```bash
  npx vitest run --config tests/probes/vitest.probes.config.js
  ```

  (118 files; 198 tests fail as designed, 99 controls pass). Add an area
  folder or one file to run part of them. `tests/probes/README.md` maps each
  probe to its finding. When a finding is fixed, its probe becomes a
  regression test in `tests/unit/` (`docs/testing/fix-sensitivity.md`).
