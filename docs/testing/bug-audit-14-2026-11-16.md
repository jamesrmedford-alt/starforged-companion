# Fourteenth bug audit — holistic, after the hidden plans, the held result and the world snapshot — 2026-11-16

Audited commit: `cf250fb` (the merge of PR #361 — `main`; latest tag v1.8.1, so everything since is unreleased). Baseline at that commit with `vendor/foundry-ironsworn` checked out: 958 test files / 9,051 tests pass (1 skipped); lint clean; `node scripts/deadscan.mjs --strict` clean.

The maintainer asked for "a holistic bug search" after "a lot of new features". The new features are the v1.8.1 open-item sweep (issue #348), the narrative skeleton (#353), the alpha 23 playtest's second part (#354) and the world snapshot (#357); none of them had been audited or played in Foundry.

Filed as GitHub issue [#362](https://github.com/jamesrmedford-alt/starforged-companion/issues/362) (the index of every row, the decisions and the suggested order; the detail is here).

**83 findings** (AC01–AC83: 29 medium, 54 low, none high) and **14 rows that need a decision** (D1–D14). Each finding has a probe: a vitest test that asserts the correct behaviour and fails today, with at least one passing control. Nothing in `src/` was changed.

## How this audit ran

Seventeen area reviewers each read their files in full, read the diff since the thirteenth audit's fixes (`18d258b..cf250fb`: 120 `src/` files, +16,489 / −2,125) for regressions, and wrote a probe for every finding. The areas: `src/index.js` in three parts; Batch Mode with `src/multiplayer/`; the move pipeline and upgrades; the resolver, interpreter and pacing; the suffer moves and card actions; `narrator.js` in two halves; the narrator prompt and world snapshot; the hidden plans; entities and name matching; fact continuity and the X-Card; the World Journal and oracles; sessions and the Private Channel; characters, ships, art and audio; panels and sectors.

The lead swept the lines added since `18d258b` for raw `fetch`, unbounded uploads, `innerHTML` sinks, dynamic regular expressions, player-character type checks and relay kinds without a handler (all clean), then re-ran every probe file and read the decisive source lines for 37 of the findings (most of those reported from more than one area, and most of the medium rows): 107 files, 178 tests fail on their finding's own assertion — none on an import error, a timeout or a setup crash — and 157 controls pass. The reviewers reported 106 findings; the same defect found from several areas was merged, which left 83. No reviewer finding was dropped.

This is a source and harness audit, not a live Foundry playthrough, and nothing was run against the live API. Rows marked **(Foundry)** (AC76, AC77, AC83, and D1) depend on Foundry behaviour that could not be checked offline.

## Where the findings are

Most of the findings sit where this cycle's three features meet each other or older code:

- **The held result (#354 P2-10) and the hidden plans (#353) both hang work on the rolled resolution** — the plan request, the behind-the-scenes oracle rolls, the arrival line. A burn or an improvement builds a fresh resolution and carries none of it over (AC04–AC06), and the held copy puts the hidden rolls on a public card (AC15).
- **The stated stakes (#353 R1) follow the price routing, not the miss**: a weak hit that pays a price pays the stated miss (AC01), a miss that routes no price shows a cost and charges nothing (AC02), and Sojourn's chosen price ignores the stated one (AC03).
- **A plan is written by the narrator's fact block and paced by a clock, and each has failure paths the other does not see**: a reply with no fact block (AC07), a vignette that is not written (AC08), a retired plan (AC10), a clock a scene challenge shares (AC09).
- **The new name matching (#354 P2-15 – P2-17, P2-30) is used by some gates and not others**, and its near-spelling rule merges distinct names (AC17–AC23).
- **23 rows are incomplete fixes** of rows recorded as fixed — PT03, PT08, PT13, PT14, P2-16, P2-25, P2-30, P2-31, AB08, AB10, AB11, AB23, Z14, X18 among them — where a second path was left on the old behaviour.

## What was traced and found sound

- **The free-flow move lock, claim to every exit**, including the new no-move, narrate-only and held paths: every return reaches the `finally`; a claimed source card is handed back on every not-rolled path.
- **The held result's own state machine**: Keep, the roller's next line, a chosen consequence and a later move all settle it once, pay the held price once and narrate once; its state is on the card and survives a reload.
- **Batch Mode's relay handlers and locks**: each entry action takes the requester from the relay's context, never from the payload; every driver lock and in-flight flag releases in a `finally`.
- **The suffer mechanics**: what each move reduces, the overflow past 0, the resist roll and its miss choice, the `assetDeltas` receipts a burn undoes, the one-shot card buttons, the legacy arithmetic.
- **The plan store**: no plan write runs on a player's client; the Secrets journal is made by a GM with no player permission; the store's own cards carry a plan id and no text; the World Journal panel's Secrets section and the "has a plan" markers are built on a GM's client only (the rolls on a held card are AC15).
- **The lines added since the thirteenth audit**: no raw `fetch`, no unbounded upload, one `innerHTML` sink (escaped), one dynamic `RegExp` (escaped), every requested relay kind has a handler.
- **Issue #344's four rows** (A01–A04) are unchanged in the code: still open, neither fixed nor worse.

Each reviewer's full list is in [`bug-audit-14-coverage.md`](bug-audit-14-coverage.md).

## Decisions

| ID | Code | Question | Options | Raised by |

| D1 | SECRETS-JOURNAL-CLIENT-READ | **(Foundry)** The code and the records disagree on whether a player's client can read the GM-only Secrets journal. `listSkeletons()` has no GM check and runs on players' clients (the Private Channel's story, narration on a player's own key, the Session panel's What's open); `oracleConsult.js` (P2-31), SNAPSHOT-PLAYER-KEY and `worldSnapshotStore.js` assume a player's client cannot read it. Live check, in a player's console: `game.journal.getName("World Journal — Secrets")?.pages.contents.map(p => p.flags["starforged-companion"])` | SESS, NARA, NARB, SKEL |
