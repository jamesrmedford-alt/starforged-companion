# Thirteenth bug audit — serious bugs, stalls and locks — 2026-11-11

Audited commit: `53b4150` (the merge of PR #346, the twelfth audit's fixes — `origin/main`; latest tag v1.8.0, so the #345 fixes are unreleased). Baseline at that commit, with `vendor/foundry-ironsworn` checked out: 775 test files / 7,828 tests pass (2 skipped); lint clean.

The maintainer asked for a review of the codebase "for serious bugs and stalling or locking conditions". This audit is narrower than the twelfth: it reports only high and medium findings, and looks first at every guard, lock, in-flight flag, promise chain, queue and awaited dialog. Low-severity UX, wording and doc drift were not collected. Nothing in `src/` was changed. No GitHub issue has been filed.

**24 findings** (AB01–AB24: 1 high, 15 medium, 4 that need a live two-GM check, 4 low) and **3 rows that need a decision** (D1–D3). Each has a probe: a vitest test that asserts the correct behaviour and fails today, with at least one passing control.

## How this audit ran

- **Ten area reviewers** each read their files in full: `src/index.js` in two halves (the chat dispatcher and move lock with `src/multiplayer/`; outcome effects, payoffs, hooks and `ready`), Batch Mode, the move pipeline and upgrades, the suffer moves / riders / dialogs, narration and pacing, fact continuity with the X-Card and World Journal, entities with art and characters, sessions with clocks / Private Channel / audio, and panels with sectors and oracles. Each traced every guard from claim to every exit.
- **Two regression reviewers** read the fix diffs since the twelfth audit's base (`22bdbeb..HEAD`: 58 `src/` files, +3,526 / −710) hunk by hunk — the lock, pipeline and round code; and narrator memory, entities, journals and panels.
- **The lead** traced the two shared primitives (the campaignState write chain and the GM relay queue) and swept the tree for raw `fetch`, dialog waits, loops and nested chain or relay calls; then read the cited source for every reviewer finding and re-ran every probe file. Each probe fails on its finding's own assertion — none on an import error, a timeout or a setup crash — with its controls passing.
- One reviewer finding was dropped as a recorded trade-off (the Chronicle's per-character write chain has no timeout — `decisions.md`, "Trade-offs accepted", U57), with its probe. Same-shape findings from different reviewers were merged.
- This is a source and harness audit, not a live Foundry playthrough. Rows marked **(Foundry)** depend on Foundry behaviour that could not be checked offline.

## What was traced and found sound

- **The campaignState write chain** (`src/multiplayer/campaignStateChain.js`): every one of its ~60 call sites runs a task with exactly one awaited write and no nested chain call; a rejected task does not poison the chain; a hung write is passed after 10 s by the writes *behind* it.
- **The GM relay queue** (`src/multiplayer/gmRelay.js`): released in a `finally`; a hung handler is passed after its budget (60 s, or 270 s for a Claude-calling kind); no handler awaits a nested relay request.
- **The free-flow move lock, claim to every exit** (`src/index.js:992–1119`): every return and throw reaches the `finally`; a twice-refused release tells the GMs.
- **Network calls**: every `fetch` in `src/` goes through `fetchWithTimeout` (120 s, body included). The only unbounded network calls are Foundry's own uploads (AB02).
- **Dialogs**: `MoveConfirmDialog` and `ClarificationDialog` settle on every close; every `DialogV2.wait` either passes `rejectClose: false` or is caught; the rider prompt runs after the lock's release.
- **Dice animation** is fire-and-forget everywhere; it cannot hold the pipeline.
- **In-flight sets and busy flags** (`_inFlight`, `_upgradeInFlight`, `_picksInFlight`, `_draftsInFlight`, `_portraitsInFlight`, `_nativePayoffsInFlight`, `_rewardPicksInFlight`, `_stampInFlight`, the Round window's and sector wizard's `#busy`, the Private Channel's `busy`): each is released in a `finally`.
- **Loops**: the held-move drain, the round's advance pass and the AA08 hand-back guard all terminate; no document hook re-triggers itself.
- **Oracle tables**: every table and sub-table tiles 1–100 with no gap or overlap.

## High

### AB01 · MAKE-CONNECTION-LOCK-HELD-FOR-PORTRAIT — high · LOCK

**Where:** `src/entities/entityExtractor.js:600` (the enrichment is awaited; the bond is registered only after it, `:602`) and `:1029` (`await generatePortrait`); `src/narration/narrator.js:871` and `:1492–1503` (the capture is synchronous for a Make a Connection hit); `src/index.js:1069` (narration awaited inside the lock; `recordMove` at `:1080`, release at `:1093`); `src/art/generator.js:523` (the upload, no deadline).

**What happens.** With the defaults — an OpenRouter key set, Auto-Seed NPC off — a Make a Connection hit posts its move card and narration, then runs its capture inside the narration call: detection, `createConnection`, then the portrait (image request, an optional neutral-prompt retry, the download, the Art Cache write, the file upload). Until that returns:

- `campaignState.pendingMove` stays true: every other move at the table is refused, and card-button moves are held.
- `recordMove` has not run: the roller's meters, the move record and Burn / Improve are not applied or opened.
- The roller holds no bond.
- In Batch Mode the round stays in its narrating phase for the same wait.

Every step but the last is bounded (120 s per request — about 4 minutes for the image and its download, about 8 with the retry). The upload is not: a stalled upload holds the lock until the GM reloads, and the reload's lock reset does not run `recordMove` for that move (AB14).

**Probe:** `ent/makeConnectionHoldsMoveLockForPortrait.probe.js` — while the image is out, `pendingMove` is `true` (expected `false`) and the roller's bonds are `[]` (expected `['Sable Ortiz']`). Control (no image key) passes.

**Recorded?** No. U29 decides only who owns the portrait; `decisions.md` records the synchronous detection, not the portrait wait.

**Fix direction.** Register the bond, then start the portrait without awaiting it (it already tolerates a late link), so the capture returns once the card and bond exist. Bound the upload (AB02).

## Medium — stalls and locks

### AB02 · UPLOADS-UNBOUNDED — medium · STALL

**Where:** `src/sectors/sectorArt.js:232`, `src/art/generator.js:523`, `src/moves/shipMapArt.js:191`, `src/audio/cache.js:188` — each awaits `FilePicker.implementation.upload(...)` with no deadline. `src/net/fetchWithTimeout.js:5–11` states the rule: "Every external network call in the module must be bounded … a stall is not a throw."

**What happens.**

- **Sector creation** (`src/sectors/sectorPanel.js:313–333`, `:284`): the image fetch is bounded; the upload after it is not. If it stalls, `Promise.all` never settles, the Sector Record journal and Scene are never built, the wizard stays on "Creating sector…", and `creationsInFlight` keeps the id so a later call joins the dead run. The settlement, connection and site Actors already created remain, unlisted. Only a reload recovers, and the sector cannot be resumed.
- **Narrator audio** (`src/audio/index.js:367–387`): a clip whose cache upload never answers stays in `_synthInFlight`; every later request and Play of that clip joins the dead promise, no `audio.synth.done` is sent, and AA61's held-bytes retry is never reached. A reload synthesises — and bills — the clip again.
- **Portraits** hold `_portraitsInFlight` and the Entities panel's generating state; on a Make a Connection hit, the move lock (AB01).

**(Foundry)** Core's upload is a plain `fetch` with no timeout, as far as could be recalled; on The Forge it is an HTTPS call to the Assets Library. Not checked against Foundry source.

**Probes:** `ui/sectorCreateUploadStall.probe.js` (after 30 simulated minutes the creation has not settled and the wizard's Create is still disabled); `sess/audioSynthInFlightWedged.probe.js` (0 done signals for two later requests, expected ≥ 1). Controls pass.

**Recorded?** No. SECTOR-CREATE-PARTIAL covers the throw path, V26 the upload's result shape, AA61 a refused upload.

**Fix direction.** One helper that races an upload against a deadline and returns null on timeout, used at all four sites.

### AB03 · READY-GM-STANDS-DOWN-FOR-LOADING-GM — medium · STALL

**Where:** `src/multiplayer/gmGate.js:56–89` (`isCanonicalGM` elects among *active* GMs; `active` is true from socket connect, before that client's `ready`); consumers `src/index.js:851`, `src/multiplayer/commandRouting.js:98–107`, `src/multiplayer/gmRelay.js:487–490`; the handlers exist only after `ready` (`src/index.js:7794–7800`).

**What happens.** gm-2 is running the table, ready and keyed. gm-1 — a lower user id, keyed on an earlier night — opens or reloads the world. For the whole of gm-1's page load every client computes canonical = gm-1: gm-2's chat pipeline, relay listener and canonical-GM routes all stand down, and gm-1 has no hooks yet. A player's `!x` posts no card and records no pause; a typed line is neither paced nor rolled; card presses are dropped. `hasActiveGM()` is true, so nobody is warned, and nothing replays at `ready`.

**(Foundry)** Rests on `user.active` being true before `ready`; the module's own comment at `src/ui/settingsPanel.js:2552` says it is.

**Probe:** `idxa/readyGmStandsDownForLoadingGm.probe.js` — the X-Card is recorded neither during the load nor after (expected both); the move is rolled 0 times (expected 1). Control (gm-1 not in the keyed registry) passes.

**Recorded?** Partly. "Accepted limitations → No ack channel" (`known-issues.md`) accepts a relay or X-Card dropped while *the* GM's client is loading — the one-GM case. Nothing records that a second, ready GM stands down, nor that typed lines are dropped.

**Fix direction.** Elect among GMs that have announced ready (the relay-ready broadcast exists), falling back to active GMs only when none has.

### AB04 · STALE-LOCK-HOLDER-RELOADED-NOT-CANONICAL — medium · LOCK

**Where:** `src/index.js:7565` (the whole ready reset, including "this client's own earlier page", sits inside `if (isCanonicalGM())`); `:1210–1217` (`staleMoveLockAtReady` treats any holder who is `active` as mid-move); `:1162–1167` (`releaseLockOfDisconnectedGM` acts only on a departure a ready canonical GM sees). Each recovery assumes the other covers the case.

**What happens.** Two GMs; gm-1 is canonical whenever connected. gm-2 runs a night alone and the browser closes mid-move: `pendingMove: true, pendingMoveBy: gm-2` stays. Next session both open the world in overlapping load windows. gm-1's `ready` sees the holder active — "connected and resolving a move — not reset". gm-2's `ready` is not canonical, so the reset block is skipped. Every typed move is refused with a toast on gm-1 only; forced card moves are held. Only gm-2 disconnecting or reloading while gm-1 is ready releases it; reloading gm-1 does not. Nobody is told.

**Probes:** `idxa/staleLockHolderReloadsNonCanonical.probe.js`, `idxb/reloadedLockHolderNotCanonical.probe.js` — `pendingMove` stays `true` in both boot orders (expected `false`); a player's move afterwards is refused. Controls pass (gm-2 alone frees it; the canonical GM's own old lock is freed; gm-2 leaving frees it).

**Recorded?** No. Y07 introduced "connected ⇒ live"; `tests/unit/readyResetLiveLock.test.js` pins it only for a holder that has not reloaded.

**Fix direction.** A client clears a lock *it* holds at its own `ready` whether or not it is canonical — its page has no pipeline running.

### AB05 · WRITE-NEVER-ANSWERED-WEDGES — medium · LOCK

One fault, four unbounded waits. **(Foundry)** A document or setting write has no timeout of its own: when the connection drops between a write's emit and its answer, the promise never settles, and a reconnect does not reload the page — recalled, not checked against Foundry source. The module already treats this as part of its fault model — the campaignState chain (10 s), the relay queue (60 / 270 s), the scene start (10 s) and the vignette waits (60 s) all bound it. These four do not, and each stays wedged until the GM's (or player's) browser reloads, with nobody told:

- **The move lock's claim and release** — `src/index.js:2219–2235`, `:2312`. `_moveLockClaimInFlight` is cleared only in a `finally` after its own write. Unanswered, every later move on that GM answers "A move is already being resolved" though the world's lock is free; held card moves never drain; a Batch round waits forever. Probe: `idxa/lockClaimWriteNeverAnswered.probe.js` (five minutes on: 0 move cards, 1 busy toast; expected 1 and 0).
- **Begin / End a Session** — `src/safety/sessionLifecycleDialogs.js:156`, `:198–202`, `:548`, `:575–579`. `_beginChain` / `_endChain` hold "even past [the relay queue's] wait cap" by design; behind a handler that never settles, every later Begin (End) never runs — no card, no state change. Probe: `sess/beginEndChainWedged.probe.js`.
- **The rolling summary** — `src/narration/narrator.js:2427–2435`, `:2528`, `:2542–2563`. `_summaryRefresh` never clears; STORY SO FAR is never regenerated on that GM, and `finalizeSessionSummary` awaits it, so the next Begin a Session and End a Session hang with it. The AA21 fix (`f08bd8b`) widened this: before it, a session ended with a stored summary skipped the finalise at Begin (`src/session/lifecycle.js:376–383`). Probe: `narr/summaryRefreshWedgedByHungWrite.probe.js`.
- **A player's Private Channel transcript** — `src/private-channel/transcript.js:389–396`, `:498–501`. Every later turn queues behind the hung page write and stays in memory; `isTranscriptUnsaved` stays false, so the window never says "not saved yet". A reload loses every turn since. Probe: `sess/transcriptChainWedged.probe.js`.

A *refused* write is handled correctly at all four (each probe's control).

**Recorded?** The same shape is a recorded trade-off for the Chronicle's and the Progress Tracks' write queues (`decisions.md`, U57) — though the tracks queue is awaited inside the move lock (`src/index.js:1728–1735`, `:1948`, `:2059–2064`), so a never-answered track write there holds the table's lock too. The four above are not recorded.

**Fix direction.** A shared "settle within N seconds, else warn and go on" wrapper (`settledWithin` in `sessionLifecycleDialogs.js` is already one) around each wait on a prior link, as the chain and the relay queue do. Worth revisiting U57 for the tracks queue.

### AB06 · CLAIMED-CARD-FORCED-MOVE-DROPPED — medium · LOCK (dead card)

**Where:** the claim sites — `src/moves/cardActions.js:218`, `:340`, `:430`, `:523`, `:608`, `:740`, `:855`, `:1326`, `:1504`; `src/moves/sufferCard.js:597–607` with `src/moves/sufferDialog.js:387`; `src/session/swearVow.js:121–136`, `:393`. The exits that roll nothing and hand nothing back — `src/index.js:1050–1051` (confirm closed or "Re-interpret"), `:1011` (a first lock claim refused), `:969–973` (the no-character notice), `:1101–1106` (any exception before the roll), `:1454` (the held-move drain runs only on the canonical GM).

**What happens.** A card button stamps its claim on the card (`resolved`, `milestoneMarked`, `fulfilAttempted`, …), then posts a forced move. The claim is released only if that *post* fails. When the pipeline then does not roll it, the card reads as taken on every client and its effect never happened:

- **The GM closes the confirm dialog** (✕, or "Re-interpret", which has nothing to re-word for a button). An owed Endure Harm at 0 health is not rolled, its card says "Applied", and the move's Burn Momentum has been closed for it. For the waypoint oracle pick, the ordinary reward's card has already been retired as well, so both rewards are lost.
- **The inciting card's ⚔ Swear this vow, pressed while another move holds the lock.** Its post carries no `deferWhileBusy`, so it is dropped with a toast on the GM's screen only, and the presser's button stays disabled until a reload — it has no 15 s re-arm.
- **A GM handover while a move is held.** A held move lives in the holding GM's memory; when a lower-id GM joins, the old GM's drain stops running and the new GM never held it. The move rolls later, out of the blue, when the first GM is canonical again.

**Probes:** `movesa/claimedCardConfirmClosed.probe.js` (`milestoneMarked` stays `true`, vow ticks 0); `movesb/owedResistRollLostAtConfirm.probe.js` (not rolled, not reopened, nobody told; Burn closed); `sess/incitingSwearDroppedAtLock.probe.js` (not sworn, button dead, presser not told); `idxa/heldForcedMoveStrandedOnHandover.probe.js` (0 ticks when the lock frees, 8 after gm-1 leaves). Controls pass.

**Recorded?** The neighbours cover only *held* moves: W02 (held at the lock), X14 (the session gate), AA48 (re-held after a refused claim), FORCED-MOVE-HOLD-RELOAD (open: lost on reload — the handover case is its sibling). MOVE-BUSY-NOTICE (open) is a typed line, whose author can retype. `tests/unit/faultMatrix.test.js:265` pins a closed confirm for a typed line only.

**Fix direction.** When a forced move carrying a card claim leaves the pipeline unrolled, release the claim (the card's id is on the message) and whisper the roller; give the inciting swear's post `deferWhileBusy`.

## Medium — serious bugs

### AB07 · LEGACY-TRACK-RESET-BY-LAGGING-SHEET — medium · LOST-UPDATE

**Where:** `src/multiplayer/sharedLegacies.js:111–142` (a sheet edit's value is taken as the shared track's new absolute value, then mirrored to every PC); `src/index.js:7807–7813` (sheets are brought to the shared tracks only by `backfillLegacySheets()` at the canonical GM's `ready`); `:2745–2761` (the `createActor` hook for a PC does not mirror them). The vendor sheet's ▸ is always shown and writes `current + 1` from that sheet's own value (`vendor/foundry-ironsworn/src/module/vue/components/legacy-track.vue:17–22`, `:124–128`).

**What happens.** The crew's Quests legacy stands at 24 ticks. A new PC is made after the GM's load — a new player, a replacement character; its sheet says 0. Its player clicks ▸ once. The canonical GM's hook sets the shared track to 1 and mirrors 1 to every sheet; each PC's earned XP on that track drops with it. Nothing recovers it: the next backfill takes the maximum, which is now 1 everywhere. Importing an older export onto a PC, or a PC whose mirror write was refused, takes the same path.

**Probe:** `idxb/newPcLegacyClickResetsCrewTrack.probe.js` — shared ticks actual 1, expected ≥ 24. Control (the same ▸ on an in-step sheet → 25 for everyone) passes.

**Recorded?** No. LEGACY-SHEET-DECREASE (open) is a different mechanism; `decisions.md` (#326 D3) says a sheet edit "sets the shared track to it" without considering a sheet that lags.

**Fix direction.** Mirror the shared tracks onto a PC at creation; and in the hook, treat an edit from a sheet that was behind the shared track as a delta (or re-mirror and refuse it). Shared supply has the same lagging-sheet shape (`sharedSupply.js:87–98`), with smaller stakes.

### AB08 · SHIP-INTEGRITY-UPGRADE-LOSS — medium · DATA-LOSS (unreleased fix)

**Where:** `src/entities/ship.js:349–352` (`updateShip` sets the record's `integrity` to the sheet's value on every write that does not itself set integrity); `src/entities/registry.js:201–202` (every read overlays the sheet's value). Introduced by `6821f28` (AA20). No load-time pass touches integrity.

**What happens.** Up to v1.8.0 the module read and wrote a command vehicle's integrity only on its own ship record; a loss never reached the sheet's Starship asset. A world that took three losses has record 2, sheet 5. On the AA20 code every reader says 5/5, and the next ship write of any kind — a position move, `!at`, a token drag — overwrites the record with 5. Neither store then holds the damage; nothing is logged or said. Every command vehicle carrying the Starship asset is affected.

**Probe:** `regr2/commandVehicleIntegrityUpgradeLoss.probe.js` — `getShip().integrity` actual 5, expected 2; after an unrelated position write `min(record, track)` actual 5, expected 2. Control passes.

**Recorded?** No. The AA20 row and its residuals (STARSHIP-ASSET-CONDITIONS-UNSYNCED, ENTITY-PANEL-ASSET-TRACK-STALE) do not mention a world whose two stores already differ.

**Fix direction.** Which store is right for old data cannot be known (a player may have marked only the sheet). A one-time reconcile at the canonical GM's `ready` — take the lower, or whisper the GM both values — before the first mirror overwrite. Worth doing before v1.8.1 ships.

### AB09 · MAKE-CONNECTION-DUPLICATE-NPC — medium · DUPLICATE

**Where:** `src/index.js:978` (the move's campaignState copy, read before the lock claim and held for the whole move); every dedup gate reads it — `src/entities/entityExtractor.js:246`, `:445–448`, `:575`, `:1404–1414`; the live-actor check skips `character` Actors (`:1460`); adoption skips a card the store lists (`src/entities/registry.js:317–319`).

## Decisions

### D1 · CLARIFY-HOLDS-LOCK-AFTER-ROLL

**Where:** `src/moves/movePipeline.js:1359–1364` — awaited after `resolveMove` (`:901`), `applyOutcomeEffects` (`:915`) and the Pay the Price card (`:1332`), before `postMoveResult` (`:1429`), with the lock held (`src/index.js:992`, released at `:1093`). The dialog has no timeout; closing it resolves "none" and the move goes on.

**What happens.** A hybrid move's hit whose line implies someone without naming a known entity (Face Danger, Secure an Advantage, Battle, the vow and expedition moves, Resupply) opens "Who are you interacting with?" on the canonical GM's screen. Until it is answered there is no result card, no narration and nothing recorded, and every other move at the table is refused. A GM who does not notice the prompt has locked the table.

**The record.** `decisions.md` ("Move-concurrency lock covers narration + persistence, not post-roll prompts") says: "Pre-roll dialogs (`confirmInterpretation`, `ClarificationDialog`) still hold the lock by design — they are the move's own expected confirm step, at the start, where a cancel releases the lock immediately." The clarification dialog is not pre-roll, is not at the start, and its close cancels nothing. The same entry's rule is "an interactive prompt must never hold a cross-client concurrency lock". CLARIFY-ON-GM (open) is about *who* answers it.

**Options.** (A) Ask before the roll — the relevance is resolved from the player's line, which is known then. (B) Keep it where it is and bound it (a default of "none" after N seconds). (C) Reaffirm holding the lock, and correct the record.

**Probe:** `movesa/clarificationHoldsLockAfterRoll.probe.js` (asserts A / B: the lock is free while the prompt is open).
