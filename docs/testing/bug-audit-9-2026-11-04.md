  suffer, narration and context, fact continuity and the World Journal, entities
  and character, session / clocks / pacing / safety, sectors / oracles / truths,
  multiplayer / Private Channel / audio, UI / settings / help), a cross-cutting
  sweep over the whole `src/` tree for the repo's recurring invariant violations
  (cached-by-reference mutation before a refusable write, stale whole-object

  and a rules-fidelity pass that compared the module's mechanics with the
  vendored foundry-ironsworn data and the play-kit document — plus the lead's own
  pass. Each auditor traced its area's entry points (chat commands, card
  buttons, hooks, dialogs, relays) against the rules text, the repo's invariants
  (`rules/reachability.md`, `rules/foundry-api.md`, `rules/narrator-memory.md`)
  and `docs/decisions.md`, and wrote a probe for each defect.
- Every candidate was re-verified by the lead against the source at HEAD (the
  cited lines and their callers read, every probe run) and checked against
  `docs/known-issues.md`'s open items and `docs/decisions.md`. Thirty rows whose
  status turned on intent, a rules reading or overlap with a known item were
  each handed to an independent adversarial refuter (see "Refuter pass").
- Same-shape findings from several auditors were merged into one row (X06, X09,
  X12, X24, X26).
- This is a source and harness audit, not a live Foundry playthrough. Rows
  marked **(Foundry)** depend on behaviour that could not be checked offline.

  one, the other side of the hop.
