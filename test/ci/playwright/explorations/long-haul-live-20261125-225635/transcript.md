Aboard the Kobayashi IV, a corroded water-recycler sits dark in a maintenance bay, its coolant lines hardened to chalk. Two decks up, a single plate in the hull has begun to weep rust, and no one on board has logged it. The same rot is eating the pipework at Hollow Spire, where the rationing is blamed on pirates and the raiders are blamed for everything else. Whoever is hitting the Cinder Expanse lanes has not cut these systems open. They have only waited for them to fail, which saves a great deal of ammunition.

**NARRATOR — Gamemaster**

You are alone in the hangar of the Kobayashi IV, and you have decided the room needs a lesson. Tomorrow's lesson, the one you have rehearsed twice aloud, is how to handle a cutter in a crosswind. There is no one to teach it to, so the empty bay gets it, with a pointer you found in a locker and a tone you reserve for pupils. Across the hangar the dockside hatch hangs open onto Hollow Spire, a settlement so cut off that no one has ever knocked on it by accident, and the dim corridor beyond stays politely empty. You tap the pointer against a crate, call it "a promising student," and wait for it to take notes.
