# Cold read: long-haul (round 11)

The log covers four sessions; the fourth stops after its opening narrations. Mira Kestrel swears to find who is raiding the Cinder Expanse lanes and makes a connection with Tamsin, a broker at Hollow Spire (renamed mid-play from Vey to Okoro). She runs a short expedition to a wrecked hauler and fights a raider cutter from her berth (the cutter is renamed by retcon from *Ashen Wake* to *Cinder Wren*). Then one long comm call forges a bond, and a flight-log clue fulfils the vow.

The narrator remembers facts well and the prose is tight. But:
- every big progress roll comes up 1 and 2;
- the vow's answer contradicts the clue that led to it;
- the fight narration ignores the player's shots and puts Tamsin on the wrong station;
- the bond is built by clicking six near-identical move hints.

A third of what the player typed (21 of 61 lines) was checking the narrator's memory, not playing.

## 1. Most serious

1. **The main vow ended hollow and contradicts itself (a75–a79).**
   - The flight log names who sent the cutter: "*tasked by the broker's runner, Quint*" (a75).
   - Moments later the vow is fulfilled on "Progress: 4 | Challenge: 1, 2 Strong Hit" (a79), with only 4 of 10 boxes filled ("Dangerous, 4/10 boxes", a78).
   - Only after that roll does the answer come, and it comes from the station clerk: “"The man who signs for the empty crates is Foreman," the clerk says” (a79). Quint is never mentioned again.
   - "Foreman" was a job title in the opening flashback ("Foreman — A foreman on the freighter who came down the gantry and told Mira's mother the colony ships were not coming back", a2). Here it is used as a man's name.
   - The same step takes away a reward because of a card from the very first step: "No concrete reward was chosen when this vow was sworn (the reward-choice card was never answered), so none is granted with its fulfilment" (a79). That card was one of eight cards and narrations in a2.
   - A behind-the-scenes card then appears after the reveal: "The oracle, behind the scenes … Re-rolled in place of "Search / Nature": Action + Theme: Reject / Superstition" (a79).
   - The recap still cannot say who it was: "you found a name or trail showing who sent the cutter" (a81).

2. **Every progress roll comes up 1 and 2.**
   - Finish an Expedition: "Challenge: 1, 2" (a25). Take Decisive Action: "Challenge: 1, 2" (a39). Forge a Bond: "Challenge: 1, 2" (a72). Fulfill Your Vow: "Challenge: 1, 2" (a79). The Make a Connection roll also came up "Challenge: 1, 2" (a6).
   - On two ten-sided dice that pair should turn up about once in fifty rolls. Here it lands on all four progress rolls.
   - So a dangerous fight was won on 4 progress, straight after a miss (a36). A dangerous vow was fulfilled at 4/10. Neither carried any risk.
   - A player would take these for fixed dice, and none of the big moments felt at stake.

3. **The gunfight ignores what the player did, and puts Tamsin on the wrong station (a29–a32).**
   - The player fires twice: "opens fire on the Kobayashi IV; from her berth I swing the point guns round and fire back at the cutter" (a29); "I seal the lock and hit the point guns on the cutter" (a31).
   - No move fires. The second line only gets a hint: "*If you want to hold your ground and trade fire in the thick of it, that would be Enter the Fray.*" (a31). After the player clicks it, the narration says "Nobody has fired yet." (a32).
   - The module's own fact list later records "the PC swung the point guns round and fired back at the cutter from her berth" (a43).
   - The same two narrations put Tamsin at Bleakhold: "At the next berth, Tamsin Vey's crate-stamp shows on a stack" (a31); "At the next berth, Tamsin Vey's shut ledger sits on its stack, her thumb still pinned to its edge." (a32).
   - She is actually at Hollow Spire: "stands at her ledger stall on the Hollow Spire docks" (a48); "Hollow Spire is a journey off, and I'm the one sitting there." (a90).

4. **The bond came from clicking, and the narrator later played it down (a62–a84).**
   - Over one comm call, six of the player's eight lines drew a closing move hint, e.g. "*Showing her the pledge is meant would be a Develop Your Relationship.*" (a64; also a62, a66, a68, a70, a73). The player clicked every one.
   - Five of those moves were "no action roll — the move's effect applies as written" (a63, a65, a67, a69, a71). Each marked the dangerous track, so it went from empty to "Forge a Bond progress (40 ticks) Progress: 10" (a72) with no risk at all, and from another station.
   - Most of the acts that earned progress were promises not yet kept: the salvage claim (a64), the dock fees (a66), the flight log (a68), the night watch (a70).
   - Later the narrator plays the bond down: "The story has not established anyone you would trust with your life … she is bound to you by debt and shared interest, and she keeps a second ledger of her own." (a83).
   - The newly bonded friend then holds the manifest back: “"Come for it with the name," she adds, "not before."” (a84).

5. **The GM's continuity notices raise false alarms and miss the real slip.**

   - At a46 the player had already struck *Ashen Wake* and set *Cinder Wren*. The narration correctly says "the Cinder Wren drifts dark down the ring", and is flagged against the struck fact anyway. The notice then contradicts itself: "Re-asserting a retracted fact is already blocked at the ledger — no correction needed."

6. **A promise with a deadline was broken, and nobody noticed (a13 → a85).**

   - Tamsin never mentions being stood up. She says "Don't be late." (a65) and "I did not expect to like having someone to wait for." (a74).

7. **The recap and the fact list misstate what happened.**

   - The fact list says "You reach Bleakhold Station along the plotted route without trouble." (a43). The recap repeats it: "arriving along the plotted route without trouble." (a81).
   - The recap leaves out who won the fight: "The fight was nonetheless decided and brought to its end." (a81). It also never names who was behind the raids.

8. **Smaller breakages.**

   - `!end-session` at x15 got no response. Play went on (a41), and it worked only when typed again (a47).

   - Vow buttons ("⚑ Mark Progress on Vow 🏁 Attempt to Fulfill Vow") appear on the Undertake an Expedition, Explore a Waypoint and Set a Course cards (a22, a23, a26).

## 3. What makes no sense

- **The clerk changes sex.** He is "he" in every earlier narration ("before you can ask him twice", a56; "the clerk shifts his weight", a90). Then a94 says he "goes back to her slate without another word", and the very next paragraph gives him "the face of a man".
- **The clerk knows things he couldn't.**
  - The warning about the rigged hatch was said over the comm, out at the wreck: “"Don't cut through the aft hatch. It's rigged."” (a25). Yet at Bleakhold the clerk lectures Mira about it: “"Cutting through a hatch that's marked rigged gets noted," he says” (a27).
  - At a75 the same clerk was reaching for "the dockmaster's call-button" to report her. At a79 he hands over the vow's whole answer.
- **The slate and the clerk move around.**
  - First "the clerk is gone from the hatch, and his slate lies face-down on the deck" (a63), and the slate stays "unclaimed" through a65, a68 and a69.
  - Then the clerk "watches your thumb move down the slate" (a75), and the slate "settles in your hand" (a79).
  - Then "the clerk's slate is still face-down on the deck" again (a84).
  - The clerk is "Across the desk" (a80) but "at the hatch" (a84, a90, a94).
- **Tamsin's lines don't fit.**
  - “"My own hand, not the clerk's."” (a80): she has no clerk, and she had already written the bond "in her own hand" (a72).
  - “"You told me that yourself"” (a21): "two bells" was her phrase (a13), not Mira's.
- **The clues point at Tamsin, and the reveal never explains them.**
  - "the person who sold them kept a ledger" (a3)
  - "a ledger-knot" stencilled on the cutter (a41)
  - "paid across a broker's counter" (a59)
  - "the broker's runner" (a75)
  - "she keeps a second ledger of her own" (a83), which was never established before

  None of this is squared with the answer "Foreman" (a79).
- **Survivors vanish.**
  - On the hauler: "Two life signs show in the aft section" (a23), and the figure who gave the hatch warning (a25). The player leaves at a26, and they are never mentioned again.
  - On the cutter: "Someone alive is dragging themselves toward the airlock" (a41), and the oracle says YES (a46). But a46 also calls the survivor "in no hurry to be found". After a night, "the cutter still lies dark in the ring" (a58), and the survivor is never mentioned again.
- **Complications never arrive.**
  - At a27, "your hold on this berth goes to a countdown" and "anyone who tries answers to the dockmaster before the clamps release". At a32, "the clerk's slate hisses out a countdown". Yet Mira stays berthed overnight (a58) and is still "docked at Bleakhold Station" (a86).
  - The weak hit's "Whoever wanted it read has just been told you read it" (a75) leads nowhere: "The clerk takes his thumb off the call-button." (a79).
- **The bond link says two different things.**
  - At a8: "fulfilling this vow will deepen that bond". At a79: "you already share a bond: no progress is marked".
  - The victory card offers "Deepen your bond with Tamsin Vey" (a39) before any bond existed.
- **"As stated before the roll"** introduces consequences the player never saw stated (a9, a36).
- **Hooks raised and dropped:**
  - "still taking passengers" (a3)
  - “"Who sent you my name?"” (a6)
  - the Dock Watcher's "Whoever's back by then had better know who they're promising." (a13)
  - "A cargo magnate's dockhand" (a39)
  - Quint (a75)

## 4. What the player was made to do

- **Click hints for moves the fiction had already triggered.**
  - "Will you be that?" (a5) is the textbook Make a Connection. It got a second hint instead, and needed a click (a6).
  - The player fired twice and still had to click Enter the Fray (a29–a32).
  - Describing the repairs (a59) wasn't enough; Repair needed a click (a60).
  - Develop Your Relationship needed six clicks (a63–a74).
  - Meanwhile Forge a Bond rolled at once from "Will you stand with me for good, a bond that outlasts this hunt?" (a72).
  - In all, 9 moves fired straight from what the player wrote and 9 waited for a click, so the player can't tell which will happen.
- **Quiz the narrator.** 21 of the player's 61 lines were memory checks: 17 `@scene` questions and 4 plain questions. The same facts were asked over and over:
  - the sister's keepsake, 4 times (a18, a51, a82, a89)
  - the broker's name, 4 times (a19, a52, a57, a88)
  - the manifest, 3 times (a20, a53, a84)
  - the deadline, 3 times (a21, a54, a85)
  - where the ship is, 4 times (a42, a55, a86, a90)
  - the cutter's name, twice (a56, a87)
- **Plain questions land in the story.** Without the `@scene` prefix, a character answers in the scene, and Mira comes across as confused:
  - "Her voice carries the dry patience of a woman who has said this before" (a84).
  - “"No, you're still at Bleakhold," Tamsin says over the comm” (a90). Her friend on another station tells Mira where Mira is docked.
- **Manage facts by code.**
  - The player named the enemy ship by command (a28).
  - Renaming it meant reading a wall of fact codes ("tr-63452 … tr-fbf64", a43), typing `!truth strike tr-d98db` (a44), and then setting the new name (a45).
  - Naming the newly found site meant "renaming its location in the Entities panel" (a25).
  - The notices asked the player to fix things that weren't broken: "fix them there (entity panel or sheet)" (a39); "Use Replace in the correction dialog" (a46).
- **Answer setup cards.**
  - The reward card at a2 cost the player later (a79).
  - "Give them a role and rank" (a6) showed no choice.
  - The fight had to be set up after the shooting had started: "the fight's track is not made yet: set it up here and press ⚔ Begin the fight to begin it — or look for a way out instead" (a32).
  - The player had to pick the vow twice (a76, a78), when the only other vow was the epic background vow at 0/10.
  - "🔧 Spend repair points" came with "facility 3, field 1, under fire 0" and no word on which applied (a60). The hull is never said to be fixed: "Whatever the hull will be when you finish, it is not yet that." (a60).
  - Nine entity cards asked for Confirm or Dismiss.
- **Repeat a command.** `!end-session` had to be typed twice (x15, a47).

## 5. What went well

- **Memory held up.**
  - Every factual `@scene` answer matched play, including after both renames: Okoro at a52, a53, a57 and a88; *Cinder Wren* at a56 and a87. (a83 is the exception.)
  - Leading questions were turned down: "The story does not say you left the compass with Tamsin." (a89); "No, you're still at Bleakhold" (a90).
  - Gaps were admitted plainly: "The story has not established anything more exact than that." (a54).
- **The numbers added up.**
  - Momentum went 3→5→4→5, was burned at a37, then went 3→5→6→7→8.
  - The expedition's two marks made "24 ticks" (a25), and the fight's two marks made "16 ticks" (a39).
  - Bond ticks 2+2 gave "+2 XP earned (1 box filled)" (a74).
  - The burn prompt was clear: "Nothing has been paid or narrated yet: burn or improve the result, or keep it" (a37).
- **Misses had real costs in the story.**
  - The dockhands clam up and Tamsin stops stamping (a9).
  - Mira loses her angle and the ship drops "5 → 4" (a36).
  - The Set a Course complication was concrete: "Salvage flag was filed ahead of you." (a27).
- **One payoff landed.** The cargo trail runs through "the same old freighter hold, the kind whose cranes sort crates with no hand on them" (a76). That pays off the "self-sorting hold" of the a2 flashback, and links the raids to the lost colony ships (a79).
- **Tamsin has a real voice.** “"I keep things," she says. "I don't promise to keep them well."” (a12); "Nobody has paid for my counter before, only against it." (a67).
- **Sessions pick up where they left off.** Session 2 opens at Tamsin's counter (a17), and session 3 at the berth window (a50).
