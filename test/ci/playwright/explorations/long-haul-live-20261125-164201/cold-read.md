# Cold read: "long-haul" (sessions 1–3, opening of 4)

*Seen* means it is in the transcript. *Inference* means my reading as a player.

## 1. Most serious

**1. Tamsin is in two places at once.** Her stall is on Hollow Spire (a90: "ledger stall is on the Hollow Spire docks"). The cutaway after session 2 seats her in "the observation lounge on the Hollow Spire's upper ring" (a46). In session 3 Mira is at Bleakhold. The player's own line, "I climb up to the observation lounge" (a61), finds Tamsin there, with her eyes on "the viewport and the *Cinder Wren*", a wreck at Bleakhold. The narrator goes along with it. The scene then turns into her stall ("A stall lamp ticks down the concourse", a71). Later she says "Bleakhold… Hollow Spire is where my stall sits" (a92). The closing cutaway walks her "out of the ledger stall… on the Hollow Spire's lower ring" (a94). Her travel between the two is never narrated.

**2. The vow is fulfilled but its question is never answered.** The vow is "Find who is raiding the Cinder Expanse lanes." The player's trace ends at "the station's own administration… someone who works here" (a77). The fulfilment narration then overturns that: "the invitation was written aboard your own ship, through the galley's hidden installation… let the station take the blame. Their names are still not known." (a81). Session 4 opens with Mira fussing over tape in that same ship. It says nothing about the revelation or about Tamsin (a96). *Inference:* the climax is a twist handed to the player, not something they found.

**3. A survivor vanishes.** The oracle says someone is alive in the wreck: "a hand wedged in the gap" (a44). The knocking carries on through a48, a54, a56 ("you slept through the knocking") and a57. Then the player says "I go through the Cinder Wren's wreck" (a74), and all the narration gives is "The wreck's scorched console agrees." The recap leaves the survivor out (a83). Session 3 opens by making light of them: "as you would with any neighbor who is clearly going to keep at it" (a48).

**4. Player lines play twice, and the bond is a grind.** This happens eight times: a player line gets a beat that ends in an italic move hint, the same line is posted again under the character's name, the move fires, and the beat is narrated a second time (a29→a30, a59→a60, a61→a62, a63→a64, a65→a66, a67→a68, a69→a70, a72→a73). The doubling is easy to see. a61 has "She listens without touching it… 'Who told you my name?'" and a62 has "hears the whole of it without touching the compass… 'Who?'". The spare pen is handed over three times (a69, a70, a71 "a thing given") and is back in Tamsin's hand at a73. Five Develop Your Relationship marks with no roll, all in one sitting, fill the track (a62–a70; a71 "Progress: 10").

**5. A reward is lost because a card went unanswered.** The "Choose your reward" card (a2) is mentioned again only at the end: "the reward-choice card was never answered… none is granted" (a81).

## 3. Contradictions and inventions

- The cutter's white stencil is "a berth number" (a37), and the tape says "THE NUMBER ON THE FLANK IS A LIE" (a57). When the player asks the ship's name, the stencil becomes "*Cinder Wren*, white paint" (a54).
- The ship is "not berthed… drifting clear" (a53). Then "berth clamps ring somewhere below" (a56), and an answer says "You are docked at Bleakhold" (a88). No docking is ever played.
- Tamsin says "Yes. Past the raids" (a71). Two beats later: "She does not say yes" (a73).
- The seal that is "not one you checked" is "forward" (a3, a4). Its apparent echo, "You never touched that one", is at "the aft hatch" (a59).
- The a17 cutaway takes place "since dark", but a19 opens in "the daylight" before dark.
- a48 mentions "the pile of dock-fee slips", but the only slip was left on Tamsin's counter (a12).
- Tamsin calls her "Mira" (a23). Mira never gives her name anywhere in the transcript, and Tamsin is complaining about exactly that.
- The point of view slips into third person: a19 "She sets the brass star-compass", a68 "Mira's legs line up".
- Some lines mean nothing: "a fee the lifeform's feed supplier now asks" (a17), "served the Dominion" (a94), "Fortify, in the plainest sense" (a96). The opening flashback (Vigil Reach, "the Tolerated", a2) never connects to anything.
- **Narrated as done, though the player never decided it:**
  - The session start swears the vow for her: "Mira Kestrel swears one" (a2).
  - A bare button press is narrated as "You tell Tamsin plainly that she is the one person… you would trust" (a82).
  - Tamsin's rank comes out as "dangerous" with no visible choice (a8 asks to "Give them a role and rank").
- **The recap (a83) has three errors and several gaps:**
  - "The vow was tied to Tamsin again" has no event behind it in session 2.
  - "the fight was decided in the end" never says that Mira won.
  - "You told Tamsin that the raiders listen through the… galley" was a narrated realization, not something Mira said.
  - It leaves out the missed roll when swearing the vow, the survivor, and the tape.
- The End and Begin cards still name the fulfilled vow as the focus (a94 "Quest focus", a96 "Focus from last session"). The same a96 card also says no vow is in play.
- Results are posted before their dice: "Expedition complete" (a26) and "Bond Forged" (a71) both appear before the rolls.
- **Rules a player would notice:**
  - The missed Swear an Iron Vow roll (a3) has no visible cost or choice; momentum goes from 3 to 5 by a11.
  - Momentum drops from 8 to 3 at a37 with no burn shown. *Inference:* the doubled "Marked progress once" at a35–a36 means there was a burn.
  - a81 asks for a roll "to learn the impact on your legacy", and the result is "0 momentum (now 10)" (a82).

## 4. Operating the machine

- **Having to name moves:** the in-fiction ask "Will you be that?" (a5) gets dialogue but no roll, so the player rephrases it as "I set out to make a connection" (a8). "I Hearten myself" (a58) gets narration but no move.
- **Having to say things twice:** the first "!end-session" (x15) does nothing; the second one works (a45). "Attempt to fulfill your vow" is needed twice (a80, a81). "Which vow advances?" comes up at a75 and again at a78, even though the other vow sits at 0/10.
- **Cards that must be handled in a set order:**
  - One weak hit (a30) produces three cards, with the warning "Burn momentum or improve the result first — choosing here… closes them."
  - Naming the discovered site means "renaming its location in the Entities panel" (a26).
  - The entity cards are written in table-talk, such as "The player character's sister" (a13).
  - A "Set World Truths" prompt turns up in the middle of the fight (x13).
  - Renaming the ship meant finding "tr-e7c63" in a wall of IDs and striking it (a41–a43).
- **The X-Card** (x16) is raised and cleared at once. The eavesdropping voice just before it ("Now tell me who you showed it to", a39) comes back at a58–a59 and becomes the vow's answer (a81). *Inference:* if that voice was what the X-Card was raised against, it did not stay gone.

## 5. What worked

- **A chain of clues grew out of a slip the player mentioned in passing** (a12). It runs to the stencil (a37), "Same number as the slip on Hollow Spire" (a39), the tape (a57), the scraped ledger line (a67), and the tasking filed "before your berth was ever assigned" (a74). This was the best thread in the session.
- **The seal thread held** from "It is not one you checked" (a3) to "open longer than you've owned the ship" (a59). The weak-hit repair had a real cost ("sealant runs lower", a60), and the seal carries into a96.
- **Facts the player set were respected.** The cutter named with "!truth set" (a28) arrives under that name (a29). After the renames, the new ship name is used at once (a44), and Okoro is used from x19 on. The distress call and Bleakhold, which the player brought in unannounced (a24), are taken up without friction.
- **Results matched the fiction.** The missed Strike reads "Your shots go wide" (a34). The clamps hold the ship until the fight is won (a37). The strong hit on Set a Course reads "Your supplies are untouched" (a27).
- **Tamsin has a voice of her own:** "I appraise things. I don't bless them" (a19). "Colony-ship make" (a13) quietly ties the sister's compass to the Verity's Hope vow.

## 6. Out-of-character questions

Recall was right every time:
- the compass (a20, a49, a84), the manifest (a22, a51), the promise (a52, a87), and both renames (a55, a89)
- false premises were caught: "The Kobayashi IV is not berthed" (a53); "No… what you left with Tamsin was the… cargo manifest" (a91)
- gaps were admitted plainly: "The story has not fixed that to a date or hour" (a52)
- questions asked without "@scene" got answers in character: "Your manifest has sat in this book since your first night" (a86); "Tired, or testing me?" (a92)

The repeated questions look like the player testing the narrator; none of the first answers needed correcting.

There were four flaws:
- a54 changed the stencil in order to supply the name.
- a85 quotes the narrator's own invented line from a82 back as Mira's words.
- a88's "docked" was never established.
- The narrator rejected "We are still docked at Hollow Spire" (a92) but accepted Tamsin being at Bleakhold (a61).

## 7. Session seams

The openings pick up in the right place: the counter "still hers till dark" (a19), the wreck with the knocking (a48), the patched seal (a96). But each one invents what Mira is doing, and a96 ignores the climax that had just been played (a81). The closings cut to Tamsin on Hollow Spire whatever has just happened, and their details (a blood fee, a warm shard, Dominion debts) go nowhere. The last one (a94) contradicts her being at Bleakhold moments before.
