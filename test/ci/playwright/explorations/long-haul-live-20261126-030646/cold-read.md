# Cold read: long-haul (sessions 1–4)

## 1. Most serious

**Where am I? Hollow Spire and Bleakhold run together.**
- **The trip.** Mira sets a course to Bleakhold (a26, "Token moved to Bleakhold Station"). Later a skiff hop, with no travel move, takes her to Tamsin: "The skiff clears the ring and noses down toward Hollow Spire's docks" (a60).
- **People and places move with her.**
  - Bleakhold's gantry hand, last seen "at the lock" there (a59), turns up at Tamsin's stall (a63).
  - "I go to the cutter's wreck" puts her aboard at once (a71). In the next beat Tamsin is telling her "before you go back to the cutter" (a72).
  - The stall itself moves to Bleakhold: "Who in this station owes the cutter a berth?" (a70).
  - The recap ends with her "aboard the Cinder Wren at her command console" (a77), though she is at the counter.
- **Asked straight out, the game blames the player.** Tamsin, standing at her own stall, says: "Hollow Spire is where you left me. This is Bleakhold… Why would you think we'd gone back?" (a86).

**The main vow is closed, not won.**
- **Almost no progress.** The dangerous raider vow is fulfilled at 4/10 boxes (a75). Every progress roll shows challenge dice 1, 2 (a25, a35, a68, a75). So an expedition finishes at 3/10 and a dangerous fight at 4/10.
- **No "who".** The answer comes as Tamsin's summary: "No name is written yet. Who sits at the head of that trade stays unsaid" (a75).
- **The workings show.** A card then shows the answer was an oracle roll that had been re-rolled: "Re-rolled in place of 'Debate / Greed'" (a75). The reward is withheld because the session-1 reward card "was never answered" (a75).

**The character's own quest is shelved.**
- Setup adds "Find the lost colony ship Verity's Hope", and the first scene dwells on it (a2).
- Begin a Session calls it "a background vow" and swaps in a generated raider vow (a2).
- It is still "Epic, 0/10 boxes" at a74, and still "background" at a90.

**What a miss costs just disappears.**
- "The hull knocks a third time, nearer" (a3) is never heard again.

- "Your supplies thinned" (a25) comes with no supply card. The truth list and the recap still record it as fact (a39, a77).

## 4. Operating the machine

- **The repost loop.** The narrator answers an action with an italic move tag instead of a roll. The player has to re-send the same line as a table post, and the beat is narrated twice. This happens eight times: a5→a6, a28→a29, a55→a56, a57→a58, a60→a61, a62→a63, a66→a67, a69→a70. By a65 the player is writing "…to Develop Your Relationship" into the fiction. Other prose triggers moves directly (a9, a22, a32, a68, a71), so the player can't tell which will happen.
- **The fight is set up after it starts.** Enter the Fray has already been rolled when the card says: "the fight's track is not made yet: set it up here… or look for a way out instead". It comes with a rank picker and a vow picker (a29).
- **The momentum burn never shows** (a33–a35). "Marked progress once" appears twice, and momentum drops from 7 to 3 with no burn card.
- **A failed command and a stray prompt.** The first `!end-session` does nothing (x15). "Set World Truths ▸" pops up in the middle of the fight (x13).
- **A false alarm.** "The narrator may have contradicted an established fact… The cutter is now visibly dead" (a37). That matches how the fight ended. The real location breaks get no flag.
- **`!truth list`** is a wall of `tr-` ids. It files the Bleakhold docking under "inside the dead hull" (a39).
- **Cards that read wrong:**
  - vow buttons on Expedition and Set a Course (a22, a26);
  - "Attempt to Finish the Fight" on a miss with no progress (a32);
  - "Tamsin Okoro marked completed" (a68);
  - Repair's "facility 3, field 1, under fire 0. Same spend options", with the points never spent (a56);
  - "As stated before the roll" for prices the player never stated (a9, a24, a32);
  - "fulfilling this vow will deepen that bond" (a8), which later becomes "no progress is marked" (a75);
  - "End a Session again to note another quest" (a88);
  - the Ashen Wake, a ship, filed as a Faction (a28).

## 5. What worked

- **The cutter fight.** "The cutter simply is not where the scope said it would be… the return fire comes down the docking ring in a hot white line" (a32). Then "Bolts stitch her flank and tear a gout of white fire from the drive housing" (a34). The miss cost the ship integrity (5→4), and the prose showed it.
- **The boneyard.** "A spread of bone-white ribs, vast and slowly turning, the remains of something that once swam" (a22), and then "The distress call stops mid-word" (a23).
- **The oath.** "The metal takes the heat of your hand and gives none back, which is the only kind of witness a vow like this ever gets" (a3).
- **Tamsin's voice.** "I keep ledgers, not secrets" (a12). "Cargo doesn't keep a tide, Kestrel. It keeps whoever owes it" (a13). "Don't make me regret the ink" (a68). The run from a57 to a70 builds honestly to Forge a Bond.
- **Renames stuck at once:** Cinder Wren from a42, Okoro from a44.
- **The water thread paid off:** "last fuelling was paid in water-trade favours" (a71).
- **A fitting price for the a9 miss:** "One man names a lane that is plainly wrong, and pockets the credits" (Supply 5→4).
