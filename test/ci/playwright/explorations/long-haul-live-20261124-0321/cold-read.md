1. WHAT THE SESSION WAS LIKE TO PLAY

Overall: a good first sitting and a decent fight, then a story that stops moving. Session 3 is 16 memory-check questions and 2 typed actions in one room, and the quest ends without its answer. Session 4 is one opening paragraph (a78) and the page ends.

Where it flowed:
