# Cold read: long-haul (round 10)

Four sessions with Mira Kestrel: Hollow Spire docks, a distress-call detour, a dock-ring fight at Bleakhold Station, and a long comm call that ends in a bond and the vow being fulfilled. Session 1 plays well. From session 2 on, the scripted player mostly quizzes the GM and repeats the same move, and the narration falls into a loop of a few stock gestures. The low point is a77, where the narrator's own editing note appears in the story.

## 1. Most serious

1. **The narrator's drafting note is printed in the story (a77).** The bond-roll narration opens: 'The draft says nothing in your voice. The break is Okoro's "Hold the line," which has her echoing the player's earlier words. I'll cut it and let her act and speak otherwise.' It mentions "the draft", "the player" and "I'll cut it", then goes straight back into fiction ("Okoro's pen sets down."). Nobody at the table should ever see that.

2. **The customs clerk changes sex mid-scene (a47 → a53).** She is "her" from a26 to a43 ("The clerk takes her palm off the curled page", a43). Session 3 picks up the same gesture: a47 "The clerk's palm is still flat on the curled page". Then a53 'The clerk lifts one finger from the page and does not look up. "Cinder Wren," he says'. The clerk stays "he" from then on: a55 "a slip pinched in his fingers", a60 "The clerk's eyes drop to his slip".

3. **The answer to the main vow is contradicted by the recap and feels unearned (a71–a78).**
   - The culprit, Talia Tolari, appears for the first time at the moment of fulfilment. Okoro supplies the name: '"Talia Tolari," Okoro says, reading it off the page you sent her' (a75). Mira found the countersign on a station terminal (a71), and nothing shows her sending it. What she sent was the tally, Ilse's notes and coordinates (a59, a65, a69).
   - A few lines later the !recap names someone else: "The raids are run by the foreman who once collected on your family's debt … He began them to gain power over the lanes … The one behind it is Talia Tolari" (a78). The narration had said "one hand behind all of it, one name on the lanes" (a75). Nothing in play showed the foreman running raids or wanting power.
   - The recap's motive matches the oracle pair that the visible "behind the scenes" card says was thrown out ('Re-rolled in place of "Begin / Power": Action + Theme: Weaken / Hate', a75), not the replacement.
   - The vow was rolled at "Dangerous, 4/10 boxes" (a74) and cleared on challenge dice 1, 2.

4. **The narrator decides what Mira thinks and does at the start of every session, in a jokey voice that doesn't match the story.**
   - a2: "You had spent the lull collecting every small fresh thing … like guests at a very quiet party".
   - a17: "You do the sensible thing and let it hang … you will push everything a little further down the road, one small provocation at a time, and call it destiny". This reads like the narrator's own pacing plan put in Mira's head.
   - a47: "What you are doing, in fact, is disagreeing with a pen … you have decided … that you are against signing it".
   - a91: "which suits you, because you have found something to do instead. You are building a warning."
   - Each time the player had typed only !begin-session.

5. **Who holds the manifest is never settled (a3, a12, a20, a78, a81).**
   - The foreman "says he has the manifest, the one with your signature already on it" (a3).
   - Shortly after, Mira hands "the manifest" to Vey, who "looks at the factory seal, then at the signature" (a12).
   - The @scene answer admits it can't tell: "the story has not said whether that is the same document or a second one" (a20).
   - The recap then states both: the foreman "still holds the manifest with your forged signature as leverage" (a78), while Okoro says "It's right here. It hasn't moved." (a81).

6. **A survivor confirmed by the oracle disappears (a43 → a47, a55).**
   - The oracle says YES. Narration shows "a figure hauling itself along a bulkhead, one arm pressed to its ribs … It is still trying to reach someone." (a43).
   - Next session the lock is empty: "hangs adrift with a lock still standing open" (a47). After a night's rest: "dark now, her lock still open" (a55).
   - Nobody, not the clerk, the station or the narrator, mentions the injured person again.

7. **The X-Card made no visible difference (x16).**
   - The card promises "Current content is suppressed. The story will redirect at the next narration beat."
   - The next beat (a43) stays in the same scene, with the same open lock and the clerk's palm on the curled page.
   - The last thing narrated before the X-Card was the flat voice ("I know what is in it anyway.", a38). It is back at a53: "A flat voice crackles over the station comm, asking again what you carry."

Okoro *is* at Hollow Spire. The card then tells the player to fix the location with `!at &lt;place&gt;`, showing a raw HTML escape.

    - Never answering a question put to Mira: "Who's your friend?" (a13), "So how long is that, exactly?" (a21), "Give me a name." (a25), "Which one is true?" (a26), "Whose hand is that?" (a55), and Okoro's "who else knows where you're berthed" (a87, still "has not been answered" at a91).
    - Typing "!end-session" (x15), getting no response, and playing on (a38).
    - Resting "until the next morning" (a55) while the survivor waves from the lock.

11. **Session 3's comm call is a dead loop of about 12 steps (a58–a72).**
    - Mira makes six escalating offers: the tally (a58), "my own neck" (a60), the credit chit (a62), Ilse's notes (a64), flying out (a66), the coordinates (a69).
    - Each gets the same italic Develop Your Relationship offer and a no-roll mark (a59, a61, a63, a65, a67). The bond is forged at "Progress: 10" (a68), over a comm, after one meeting.

## 3. Smaller oddities

- **Narration voice.**
  - The foreman, on comm, reacts to a gesture he can't see: "I wouldn't put that on the hull plating, if I were you." (a3).
  - The a2 opener is in past tense ("were built … held one … You looked at it for a while") while everything else is present. It also starts "A flashback, then:", echoing the vignette card.
  - Sessions 1 and 4 open with two narrator posts each, in clashing tones (a2; a91, ominous red lights and then the comic sign).
- **Vey at her stall.**
  - Vey's pencil ("pencil still moving", a4; "Vey sets the pencil down", a5) becomes a pen from a45 on.
  - "She tucks it under the ledger without counting it." (a10): it is a fee slip.
  - "does not touch either" and then she "slides the sheet beneath the ledger, a page you have not seen her open" (a12).

  - "a third lock that looks decorative until you notice nobody has ever dusted it" (a17): the logic doesn't follow, and the locker never comes back.

## 4. What worked

- **Memory answers are accurate across sessions.**
  - The compass every time (a18, a48, a79, a86).
  - The manifest with Tamsin (a20, a50, a81).
  - "before the next tide of cargo" (a21, a51, a82), with an honest "The story has not said when that tide comes in." (a51).
  - Bleakhold as the location (a39, a52, a83).
- **False premises are refused cleanly.** "No. What the story records you leaving with Okoro is the cargo manifest" (a86). 'No, you are still at Bleakhold Station … "Wrong station," she says. "Though I'll take the thought."' (a87) corrects the player in the fiction and in character.
- **Plain questions without @scene get answers inside the fiction.** Vey: '"Before the next tide of cargo," she says. "Your words, not mine."' (a21). The clerk reads the name off a slip (a53). Okoro: "It's right here. It hasn't moved." (a81).
- **Retcons are honoured at once.** The cutter is the *Cinder Wren* in every named mention after a42 (a47, a53, a55, a84) and in the recap. "Okoro" is used everywhere after the rename (a45 on).
- **Vey/Okoro has a real voice.**
  - "Nobody calls me Tamsin unless they owe me or want something." (a4)
  - "Because you asked, not offered" (a6)
  - "I've heard that promise from people who meant it and people who didn't. Same sound." (a13)
  - "Fly here, and the hook man's friends see you coming" (a66)
  - '"Not today," she tells it, low and level' (a77)
- **The flashback seeds pay off.**
  - The factory-sealed manifest and the foreman "who has collected from your family before" (a2) come back on the comm (a3).
  - The seal turns up on the wreck's crates: "You have seen that stamp before, on a manifest." (a23). And on the cutter: "Stamped hull seal on the manifest, same as the crates." (a53).
- **The handwriting clue builds well.** "It leans left, hooked at the tail, and it is nearly familiar." (a56), then "the signing hand looks very like the one on the crates" (a71), then "the hand does not change" (a72).
- **Results are narrated faithfully.**
  - The vow-roll miss becomes the foreman's call: "you should come collect your name" (a3).
  - The pre-stated price on the Strike miss is narrated as given: "Your shots go wide" / "her guns find the berth" (a33).
  - The burned-momentum upgrade lands: "engine cowl blooms white" (a35).
  - The oracle YES produces a survivor (a43).
- **The narrator waits for the player's move** instead of resolving declared actions itself: "Firing back from the berth … would be Enter the Fray." (a28); "Setting the damage right in earnest would be a Repair." (a56).
- **Continuity across session breaks.** Session 2 resumes on "Vey's question hangs there" (a17). Session 4 remembers "Okoro's question about who else knows your berth has not been answered" (a91).
- **Small callbacks and useful table cards.** The vow is sworn "to the mug and the apple ration and the blanket that smells of nothing" (a3). The "Fulfill Your Vow — which vow? … nothing has been rolled yet" card (a74) clears up the ambiguity without costing a roll.
